Piko Turbo

Before this branch the repo was a one-line README. After it, Harrison gets a static French platformer at the repo root. No lives, no game over. A fall or a bump says “Oups !” and returns to the last flag with every ring kept.

How a round goes

1. TitlePiko Turbo, Jouer or Niveaux
▶
2. LevelLa prairie, La colline, or Le looping
▶
3. RunArrows or the big tablet buttons. Space or Saut jumps.
▶
4. OupsPond or orange blob respawns at the flag. Rings stay.
▶
5. BravoThe star banner. Next level, replay, or the list.

What is in the levels

La prairie

A short hop over a smiling pond, then a grass slope and an orange speed pad.

La colline

Two hills, one slow blob, a speed pad before a wider pond.

Le looping

The pad is fast enough to take the circle. Walking speed stays on the ground and still reaches the star.
