Short landscape, start button on screen

Florian’s iPad and mobile Safari, held sideways, showed Piko Turbo, Le hérisson bleu, and Pour Harrison, then stopped. The Jouer button sat below the visible area, and the page could not scroll.

Before and after

Before

min-height: 100dvh let the title grow past the screen. overflow: hidden blocked scrolling. At 1024×470, Niveaux ended near y=539.

Piko Turbo Jouer cut off
After

The page height is the visible viewport. A short screen uses a side-by-side title, and a scale keeps every menu inside. A tap anywhere on the title starts level 1, except Niveaux.

Piko Turbo Jouer

What changes on resize

  1. js/viewport.js sets --app-height from visualViewport, because Safari’s layout viewport stays taller than the area you can see.
  2. Title, level list, and Bravo scale with transform only when they are still too tall.
  3. Phaser.Scale.FIT and CENTER_BOTH redraw the canvas. The left, right, and Saut buttons stay in the bar under it.
  4. Listeners: resize, orientationchange, visualViewport resize and scroll, and a ResizeObserver.

Risk

The menu scale is a safety net for heights the compact layout does not already fit. A very short window shrinks the title instead of scrolling. The game canvas stays on Phaser’s own FIT scale, so the touch buttons do not shrink with that transform.
